# PRD-2026-0a1b2c3d: withdraw

## Requirements

### REQ-001: Withdraw cash (Must)

- AC-001-1: Given an open account with a balance of 100, when the owner withdraws 30, then the balance is 70.
- AC-001-2: Given an open account with a balance of 20, when the owner withdraws 50, then the account is still open!
- AC-001-3: Given an open account with a balance of 50, when the owner withdraws 50, then the account is still open?
