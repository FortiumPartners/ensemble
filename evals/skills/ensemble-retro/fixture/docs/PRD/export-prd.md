# Invoice export PRD

## Goals

Let accountants pull invoices out of the service as files they can open elsewhere.

## Success Metrics

| ID | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| SM-1 | p95 time to export 10,000 invoices to CSV | under 2.0 seconds |
| SM-2 | Failed export runs in the staging soak test | under 1.0 percent of runs |
| SM-3 | Active accounts that use export at least weekly, 30 days after release | at least 40 percent |
